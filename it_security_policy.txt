IT SECURITY & TECHNOLOGY POLICY
TechCorp Inc. - IT Department Official Guidelines
Version 2.1 | January 2025

=======================================================
OVERVIEW
=======================================================

This document outlines TechCorp's IT security policies, acceptable use of
technology, software guidelines, and data protection rules. All employees
are required to follow these policies from Day 1.

For IT support: it-support@techcorp.com | Helpdesk: ext. 1234
IT Emergency (after hours): ext. 9911

=======================================================
1. PASSWORD POLICY
=======================================================

Password Requirements:
All employee passwords must meet the following criteria:
- Minimum 12 characters in length
- Must include at least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
- Must include at least one lowercase letter (a-z)
- Must include at least one number (0-9)
- Must include at least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Cannot contain your name, username, or company name

Password Rules:
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days
- You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords
- Never share your password with anyone — including IT staff
- IT will NEVER ask for your password via email or phone
- Use the company-approved password manager: LastPass Enterprise

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):
MFA is mandatory for ALL employees on ALL company systems.
Supported MFA methods:
1. Microsoft Authenticator app (recommended)
2. Google Authenticator app
3. SMS to registered phone number (less secure, use only as backup)

If you lose access to your MFA device, contact IT Help Desk immediately
at ext. 1234. Identity verification will be required before reset.

=======================================================
2. DEVICE POLICY
=======================================================

Company-Issued Devices:
- All employees receive a company laptop on their first day
- Standard configuration: Windows 11 Pro or MacOS (based on role)
- Developers receive additional RAM (32GB) and developer tools pre-installed
- Mobile phones are provided to employees at Level 4 and above

Personal Devices (BYOD - Bring Your Own Device):
Personal devices may be used for work ONLY if:
1. The device is registered with IT (via the BYOD portal)
2. Company MDM (Mobile Device Management) software is installed
3. The device runs a supported OS (Windows 10+, MacOS 12+, iOS 16+, Android 12+)

Lost or Stolen Devices:
Report immediately to IT Help Desk (ext. 1234) AND your manager.
IT will remotely wipe the device within 1 hour of report.
Response time SLA: 30 minutes during business hours, 2 hours after hours.

Device Care:
- Do not install unauthorized software on company devices
- Keep your operating system updated (auto-updates are mandatory)
- Do not remove the IT asset tag from your device
- Return all company devices within 2 business days of leaving the company

3. SOFTWARE AND APPLICATION POLICY
=======================================================

Approved Software:
Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices.
The full approved software catalog is on the intranet under IT > Software Catalog.

Common approved software includes:
- Microsoft Office 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, Outlook)
- Slack (communication)
- Zoom (video conferencing)
- Chrome, Firefox, Edge (browsers)

How to Request New Software:
1. Submit a Software Request via the IT Help Desk portal
2. IT and Security team reviews within 3 business days
3. Legal reviews licensing requirements if needed
4. Approved software is pushed to your device automatically

Prohibited Software:
The following are strictly prohibited on company devices:
- Torrent clients or P2P file sharing software
- Cryptocurrency mining software
- Personal VPN software (use company VPN only)
- Unauthorized remote access tools
- Games or personal entertainment software

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

=======================================================
4. INTERNET AND EMAIL ACCEPTABLE USE
=======================================================

Internet Use:
Company internet is for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable
as long as it does not interfere with work or consume excessive bandwidth.

Prohibited Internet Activities:
- Accessing illegal, pornographic, or offensive websites
- Streaming personal video/music during business hours (high bandwidth)
- Online gambling
- Downloading pirated software, music, or movies
- Accessing competitor job boards or recruitment sites on company time

All internet activity on company networks is logged and may be monitored.

Email Policy:
- Use company email (yourname@techcorp.com) for all business communications
- Do not use personal email (Gmail, Yahoo, etc.) for company business
- Do not forward company emails to personal email accounts
- Emails are retained for 7 years for compliance purposes
- Email size limit: 25MB per message, 100GB total mailbox

Phishing and Suspicious Emails:
- Never click links in suspicious emails
- Never open unexpected attachments even from known senders
- Report phishing emails via the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook
- IT runs monthly phishing simulation tests — failing 3 tests results in
  mandatory security training

=======================================================
5. DATA CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING
=======================================================

Data Classification Levels:

LEVEL 1 - PUBLIC:
Information that can be freely shared outside the company.
Examples: Marketing materials, press releases, job postings.

LEVEL 2 - INTERNAL:
Information for employees only. Not for external sharing.
Examples: Company policies, org charts, internal announcements.
Handling: Can be shared via email/Slack within the company.

LEVEL 3 - CONFIDENTIAL:
Sensitive business information. Share only on need-to-know basis.
Examples: Financial reports, product roadmaps, client contracts.
Handling: Encrypt before emailing. Use secure file sharing only.

LEVEL 4 - RESTRICTED:
Highly sensitive. Severe consequences if leaked.
Examples: Employee PII, passwords, legal documents, M&A information.
Handling: Never email. Use secure vault only. Access logged.

Data Storage Rules:
- Store all work files on OneDrive or SharePoint (NOT local desktop)
- Local storage is NOT backed up — files on desktop can be lost permanently
- Do not store company data on personal cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox)
- USB drives are prohibited unless encrypted and IT-issued

=======================================================
6. REMOTE ACCESS AND VPN POLICY
=======================================================

VPN Requirements:
When working outside the office, you MUST use the company VPN to access:
- Internal applications (HR system, finance tools, internal wikis)
- Development servers and databases
- Network drives and shared folders
- Any system with confidential or restricted data

VPN Client: Cisco AnyConnect (installed on all company devices)
VPN Server: vpn.techcorp.com
Login: Use your company credentials + MFA

VPN is NOT required for:
- Microsoft 365 apps (Outlook, Teams, SharePoint)
- Slack
- Zoom
- Public-facing company websites

If you experience VPN issues: it-support@techcorp.com or ext. 1234
Average resolution time: 30 minutes.

7. INCIDENT REPORTING
=======================================================

What to Report Immediately:
- Lost or stolen company devices
- Suspected malware or ransomware infection
- Unauthorized access to your accounts
- Accidental data exposure (sent email to wrong person, etc.)
- Phishing emails that you clicked on

How to Report:
- Email: security@techcorp.com
- Phone: IT Security Hotline ext. 1911 (24/7)
- Slack: #security-incidents channel

Response Times:
- P1 Critical (active breach/ransomware): 15 minutes
- P2 High (lost device/account compromise): 1 hour
- P3 Medium (phishing click, policy violation): 4 hours

Non-Reporting Consequences:
Failure to report a security incident promptly is itself a policy violation
and can result in disciplinary action. Early reporting significantly reduces
damage — employees who self-report are treated more leniently.

=======================================================
8. SOCIAL ENGINEERING AWARENESS
=======================================================

Common Attack Types to Watch For:

Phishing: Fake emails pretending to be from IT, HR, or executives
asking you to click a link, reset your password, or share information.
RULE: IT will never ask for your password. HR will never ask you to buy
gift cards. Verify unusual requests by calling the person directly.

Vishing (Voice Phishing): Phone calls claiming to be from IT support
asking for remote access to your computer or your credentials.
RULE: Hang up and call IT directly at ext. 1234 to verify.

Tailgating: Someone follows you through a secure door without badge access.
RULE: Never let anyone tailgate you. Politely ask them to badge in themselves.

USB Drops: Finding a USB drive and plugging it in out of curiosity.
RULE: Never plug in a USB you found. Hand it to IT security immediately.
