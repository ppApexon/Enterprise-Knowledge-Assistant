EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
TechCorp Inc. - Welcome Guide for New Employees
Version 3.2 | January 2025

=======================================================
WELCOME TO TECHCORP!
=======================================================

We are thrilled to have you join our team. This handbook will guide you through
everything you need to know about working at TechCorp. Please read it carefully
and keep it as a reference throughout your time with us.

=======================================================
1. ONBOARDING PROCESS
=======================================================

Week 1 - Orientation:
Day 1: IT setup, office tour, meet your team
Day 2: HR orientation - benefits, payroll, policies
Day 3: Department introduction and role overview
Day 4-5: Start on assigned onboarding tasks

Required Training (First Month):
All new employees must complete the following within their first 30 days:
1. Security Awareness Training (online, ~2 hours)
2. Data Privacy & GDPR Training (online, ~1.5 hours)
3. Code of Conduct Training (online, ~1 hour)
4. Role-specific technical training (varies by role)

Your Manager Will:
- Assign you an onboarding buddy (a senior colleague)
- Set up weekly 1:1 meetings for the first 3 months
- Create a 30-60-90 day plan with you

=======================================================
2. OFFICE FACILITIES
=======================================================

Office Hours:
Main office is open Monday-Friday, 7 AM to 9 PM.
Access is 24/7 for employees with building access cards.

Meeting Rooms:
- Book meeting rooms via the "Room Booking" system in the company intranet
- Rooms can be booked up to 2 weeks in advance
- Please cancel bookings if you no longer need the room
- Large conference rooms (10+ people) require 24-hour notice

Cafeteria:
Open Monday-Friday: Breakfast (7:30-9:30 AM), Lunch (12:00-2:00 PM)
Subsidized meals available. Employees receive $5 daily cafeteria credit.

Parking:
Free parking is available in the company parking lot. Motorcycles park in the
designated motorcycle zone. Electric vehicle charging stations are available
(first 10 spots, first come first served).

=======================================================
3. IT AND SYSTEMS ACCESS
=======================================================

Your First Day Setup:
1. Collect laptop from IT Help Desk (Floor 1, Room 110)
2. You will receive your employee ID and temporary password via SMS
3. Change your password on first login
4. Set up two-factor authentication (mandatory)

Key Systems You Will Use:
- Slack: Team communication (invite sent to personal email)
- Jira: Project management and task tracking
- Confluence: Company wiki and documentation
- GitHub/GitLab: Code repositories (for technical roles)
- Workday: HR system for leave, payroll, expense claims
- Google Workspace: Email, Calendar, Drive

IT Support:
- IT Help Desk: it-support@techcorp.com or ext. 1234
- Self-service portal: helpdesk.techcorp.internal
- Response time: P1 (critical) - 1 hour, P2 (high) - 4 hours, P3 (normal) - 24 hours

=======================================================
4. COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES
=======================================================

Email Best Practices:
- Use clear subject lines that describe the purpose
- Keep emails concise and action-oriented
- Use "Reply All" only when all recipients need to know
- Response expected within 24 hours on working days

Slack Guidelines:
- Use #general for company-wide announcements
- Use #random for non-work conversations
- Use direct messages for private conversations
- Set your status when in meetings or unavailable
- Avoid sending messages after 8 PM unless urgent

Meeting Culture:
- All meetings must have an agenda shared 24 hours before
- Start and end meetings on time
- Document action items and share within 24 hours after meeting
- Consider if a meeting is necessary (could it be a Slack message?)

=======================================================
5. CAREER DEVELOPMENT
=======================================================

Learning & Development Budget:
Each employee receives $1,500 per year for professional development:
- Online courses (Coursera, Udemy, Pluralsight, etc.)
- Books and learning materials
- Conference attendance
- Professional certifications

Submit learning requests through Workday under "Learning & Development."
Manager approval required for amounts over $500.

Internal Mobility:
Employees can apply for internal job openings after completing 1 year in
their current role. Internal candidates are given priority in the hiring process.
Speak to your HR Business Partner to explore opportunities.

Mentorship Program:
TechCorp offers a formal mentorship program. Apply via the company intranet.
Mentors are senior employees across different departments. The program runs
for 6 months with monthly 1:1 sessions.

=======================================================
6. HEALTH AND WELLNESS
=======================================================

Gym Subsidy:
The company reimburses up to $50/month for gym membership. Submit receipts
through Workday under "Wellness Reimbursement."

Mental Health Support:
TechCorp provides free access to the BetterHelp platform (online therapy).
All employees get 12 free sessions per year. This is completely confidential.
Access via the company intranet under "Employee Wellness."

Annual Health Checkup:
All employees receive a free comprehensive health checkup once per year.
Schedule through the company's healthcare portal or call HR at ext. 4500.

=======================================================
7. PAYROLL INFORMATION
=======================================================

Pay Schedule:
Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month directly to your
registered bank account.

Payslips:
Digital payslips are available in Workday by the 25th of each month.
For payroll queries, contact payroll@techcorp.com or ext. 4501.

Taxes:
TechCorp handles all statutory deductions. Your offer letter specifies
whether your salary is CTC (Cost to Company) or take-home.

=======================================================
8. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
=======================================================

Fire Emergency:
- Fire exits are marked with green signs
- Assembly point: Main parking lot, Section A
- Fire wardens wear orange vests and will guide evacuation

Medical Emergency:
- First aid kits are on every floor near the elevator
- Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies
- Internal emergency number: ext. 9999
- First aid trained employees are identified with a green cross on their badge
