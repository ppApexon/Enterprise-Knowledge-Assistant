COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT
TechCorp Inc. - Official Policy Guide
Last Updated: January 2025

=======================================================
1. WORK FROM HOME POLICY
=======================================================

Eligibility:
All full-time employees who have completed their 3-month probation period are eligible
to work from home up to 3 days per week. Part-time employees may work from home
up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.

Equipment:
The company will provide a laptop and necessary software licenses. Employees are
responsible for ensuring a stable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps). The company
provides a one-time $300 home office setup allowance.

Working Hours:
Remote employees must be available during core hours: 10 AM to 3 PM in their local
time zone. All remote employees must attend the daily standup meeting at 9:30 AM.

Security Requirements:
- Use only company-approved VPN when accessing internal systems
- Do not work from public WiFi without VPN
- Lock your screen when stepping away from your computer
- Do not share your work laptop with family members

=======================================================
2. LEAVE POLICY
=======================================================

Annual Leave:
- 0-2 years of service: 15 days paid leave per year
- 2-5 years of service: 20 days paid leave per year
- 5+ years of service: 25 days paid leave per year

Sick Leave:
All employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Unused sick leave cannot be
carried forward to the next year.

Maternity/Paternity Leave:
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers
- Paternity Leave: 4 weeks fully paid for secondary caregivers
- Adoption Leave: Same as maternity/paternity leave policies

Emergency Leave:
Employees may take up to 5 days of emergency leave per year for family emergencies
(death of immediate family member, serious illness of dependent, etc.). This is
separate from annual leave.

=======================================================
3. CODE OF CONDUCT
=======================================================

Professional Behavior:
All employees must maintain professional behavior in the workplace and in all
company-related communications. This includes:
- Treating all colleagues with respect regardless of their position
- Maintaining confidentiality of company and client information
- Avoiding conflicts of interest
- Not engaging in discrimination or harassment of any kind

Social Media Policy:
Employees must not share confidential company information on social media. When
discussing work on personal social media, employees must clarify they speak only
for themselves. Sharing client information on social media is strictly prohibited
and may result in immediate termination.

=======================================================
4. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
=======================================================

Pre-Approval:
Any expense over $500 requires pre-approval from your direct manager. Expenses
over $2000 require approval from the department head.

Submission:
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense.
Claims submitted after 30 days may not be reimbursed. All claims require original
receipts.

Travel Expenses:
- Domestic flights: Economy class only (unless flight is over 6 hours)
- International flights: Business class allowed for flights over 8 hours
- Hotel: Up to $200 per night in major cities, $150 in other locations
- Meals: Up to $50 per day (breakfast $10, lunch $15, dinner $25)

=======================================================
5. PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY
=======================================================

Review Cycle:
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year: in June and December. New employees
receive their first review at the 6-month mark after joining.

Rating Scale:
1 - Needs Improvement
2 - Meets Some Expectations
3 - Meets Expectations
4 - Exceeds Expectations
5 - Outstanding

Salary Increments:
- Rating 3: 5-7% increment
- Rating 4: 8-10% increment
- Rating 5: 11-15% increment
- Rating 1-2: No increment, Performance Improvement Plan initiated
