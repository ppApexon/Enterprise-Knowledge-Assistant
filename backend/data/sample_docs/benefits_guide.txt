EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE
TechCorp Inc. - Complete Benefits Overview
Effective: January 2025

=======================================================
OVERVIEW
=======================================================

TechCorp offers a comprehensive benefits package to support your health,
finances, and work-life balance. This guide explains all available benefits
and how to access them.

For benefits-related questions: benefits@techcorp.com | HR: ext. 4500

=======================================================
1. HEALTH INSURANCE
=======================================================

Coverage Options:
All full-time employees and their dependents are covered under our group
health insurance plan starting from Day 1 of employment.

Plan Details:
- Provider: BlueCross BlueShield
- Type: Comprehensive PPO Plan
- Employee Premium: $0 (fully company-paid)
- Dependent Coverage: $150/month per dependent (deducted from salary)

Coverage Includes:
- Hospitalization (up to $500,000 per year)
- Outpatient consultations (100% covered at network hospitals)
- Prescription medications (80% covered)
- Dental: Up to $1,500 per year
- Vision: Up to $300 per year (glasses/contacts)
- Mental health: 20 sessions per year at $20 copay
- Maternity: Fully covered including prenatal and postnatal care

How to Use:
1. Download the BlueCross app and use your employee ID
2. Show your digital insurance card at network hospitals
3. For reimbursements, upload bills to Workday > Benefits > Submit Claim

=======================================================
2. RETIREMENT BENEFITS
=======================================================

401(k) Plan:
TechCorp offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan with company matching.

Matching Policy:
- Company matches 100% of your contributions up to 4% of your salary
- Matching starts from Day 1
- Example: If you earn $80,000 and contribute 4% ($3,200), TechCorp adds $3,200

Vesting Schedule:
- Year 1: 25% vested
- Year 2: 50% vested
- Year 3: 75% vested
- Year 4+: 100% vested (your matched funds are fully yours)

How to Enroll:
1. Log into Fidelity NetBenefits (link in company intranet)
2. Set your contribution percentage and investment preferences
3. Enrollment must be done within 30 days of joining

=======================================================
3. EQUITY & STOCK OPTIONS
=======================================================

Stock Option Plan (for eligible employees):
Senior employees (Level 4 and above) receive Employee Stock Options (ESOs).

Grant Details:
- Options are granted at the time of joining or promotion
- Exercise price is the Fair Market Value on the grant date
- Cliff: 1 year (no vesting in first year)
- Total vesting: 4 years (25% per year after cliff)

RSU (Restricted Stock Units):
- Available for all employees Level 3 and above
- Granted annually based on performance rating
- Vest over 4 years on a quarterly basis

=======================================================
4. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE
=======================================================

Life Insurance:
- Coverage: 3x your annual base salary
- Fully paid by company
- Additional optional coverage: You can buy up to 5x salary at group rates

Short-Term Disability:
- Covers up to 60% of your salary
- Kicks in after 7 days of disability
- Maximum duration: 12 weeks
- Waiting period: none (coverage from Day 1)

Long-Term Disability:
- Covers up to 60% of your salary
- Kicks in after Short-Term Disability period ends
- Maximum duration: Until age 65 or recovery
- Fully paid by company

=======================================================
5. PAID TIME OFF SUMMARY
=======================================================

Annual Leave: 15-25 days (based on tenure - see Policy Guide)
Sick Leave: 10 days per year
Holidays: 11 federal holidays + 2 floating holidays
Parental Leave: 26 weeks primary / 4 weeks secondary caregiver
Bereavement Leave: 5 days for immediate family, 3 days for extended family
Volunteer Time Off: 3 days per year to volunteer for charitable causes
Sabbatical: After 5 years of service, eligible for 4-week paid sabbatical

=======================================================
6. COMMUTER BENEFITS
=======================================================

Pre-Tax Commuter Benefits:
Save on taxes by paying for commuting expenses pre-tax.
- Transit passes: Up to $315/month pre-tax
- Parking: Up to $315/month pre-tax

Company Shuttle:
Free shuttle service is available from major transit hubs:
- Downtown Station to Office: Every 30 minutes, 7-10 AM and 4-8 PM
- Schedule available on the company intranet

Cycling Benefits:
- Secure bicycle storage available
- Shower facilities for cyclists
- Cycle to Work program: Bike purchase up to $1,000 (interest-free company loan)

=======================================================
7. EDUCATION ASSISTANCE
=======================================================

Tuition Reimbursement:
Full-time employees pursuing relevant degrees receive:
- Undergraduate: Up to $3,000 per year
- Graduate/MBA: Up to $5,000 per year
- Certifications: Up to $1,500 per year (from the L&D budget)

Requirements:
- Must be employed full-time for at least 1 year
- Degree must be relevant to your current role or career path
- Minimum grade of B required for reimbursement
- Tax implications apply (amounts over $5,250/year are taxable)

How to Apply:
Submit tuition reimbursement request in Workday at least 30 days before
the course starts. Manager and HR approval required.

=======================================================
8. EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (EAP)
=======================================================

What is EAP:
A free, confidential resource for employees and their families facing
personal or work-related challenges.

Services Include:
- 24/7 confidential helpline: 1-800-555-HELP
- Free counseling (up to 8 sessions per issue)
- Legal consultation (30-minute sessions, up to 3 per year)
- Financial counseling (budgeting, debt management)
- Child care and elder care referrals
- Crisis support services

Important: All EAP services are completely confidential. Neither your
manager nor HR will be informed of your use of EAP services.

=======================================================
10. HOW TO ENROLL
=======================================================

Benefits Enrollment Windows:
1. New Hire Enrollment: Within 30 days of your start date
2. Open Enrollment: Every year in November (changes effective Jan 1)
3. Life Event Changes: Within 30 days of a qualifying event
   (marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage)

Steps to Enroll:
1. Log into Workday
2. Go to Benefits > Open Enrollment
3. Review and select your options
4. Add dependents if applicable
5. Review summary and confirm
